About Nemtec

Built On
Integrity.

Nemtec Automotive was founded on a simple belief: people deserve honest car repair. Not upsells, not vague answers, not inflated bills. Just straight talk and quality work.

With over 25 years of hands-on mechanic experience, we've seen it all. We'll tell you what your vehicle actually needs, what can wait, and what it's going to cost — before we ever pick up a wrench.
